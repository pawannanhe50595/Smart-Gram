Understanding SmartGram

A Multi-Village Digital Twin System for Sustainable Planning

The Research Gap

Rural data regarding population, education, and utilities is often spread across disconnected platforms like national census portals and open government datasets.

SmartGram bridges this gap by unifying these sources into a predictive digital twin, enabling more effective long-term strategy.
